What is Actually True

Normal bank-to-bank UPI transfers (Person-to-Person and standard consumer QR payments to shopkeepers) remain 100% free for Indian citizens and consumers.

close

What is Completely False

There is no 5% fee, tax, or surcharge imposed on transfers over ₹2,000. Neither the Finance Ministry nor RBI has proposed any consumer charge.

history_edu

Origin of This Rumor

Malicious actors regularly recirculate distorted summaries of a March 2023 NPCI circular regarding Merchant Prepaid Payment Instruments (PPI / digital wallets like Paytm or Amazon Pay balance) with an interchange fee paid exclusively by large merchants, not consumers.
